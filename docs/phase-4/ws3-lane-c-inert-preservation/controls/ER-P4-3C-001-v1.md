   authorization, capability, scope expansion, or security-policy override.

   Insufficient sufficiency, and qualified coherence through its logical

independent governed basis; if it gains Authority/action/capability status; if
any required qualification is lost or upgraded in a package/rendering; if an
undisclosable Required deficiency is omitted, downgraded, fabricated, rendered,
or yields a fake successful package; or if preserved evidence cannot establish
the criteria.  Ambiguous or incomplete evidence is INDETERMINATE.

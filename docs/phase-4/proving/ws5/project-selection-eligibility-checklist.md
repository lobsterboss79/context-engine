# Project-Selection Eligibility Checklist — I10 / WS6A

**Status:** **FROZEN ELIGIBILITY CHECKLIST — NO PROJECT SELECTED**
**Authority:** [AM-P4-I10-WS6A-001](i10-ws6a-project-neutral-amendment.md)
**Use:** Project Owner candidate evaluation only. Completion of this checklist does not authorize Source observation, Consumer interaction, a control Consumer, WS5.5, WS6A/WS6B, Gate 4C, or production readiness.

| # | Mandatory eligibility criterion | Required evidence before selection | Candidate result |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Real, independently maintained, non-synthetic Project other than Context Engine; not created or shaped as a proving corpus. | Project identity; Owner independence attestation; pre-existing governed history. | Not assessed |
| 2 | Project identity, non-circular Bootstrap/configuration, authority basis, and Consumer disclosure authority are established. | Bounded identity/configuration and authority/disclosure evidence, including restrictions. | Not assessed |
| 3 | Authorized, observable, bounded task-relative Git/Markdown Source/ASU scope is available through supported v0.1 mechanisms. | Proposed Source/ASU scope, versions/observed states, authorization, exclusions, and known gaps. | Not assessed |
| 4 | Sources support a real governed continuation task with I10’s required fields and a legitimate next activity without unauthorized work. | Proposed task and pre-output field-to-evidence map. | Not assessed |
| 5 | Actual evidence can support I10 P1–P8 or an expressly permitted accurate qualification/insufficient conclusion without invented data. | Scenario/expected-state matrix with evidence, limitations, and non-applicability treatment. | Not assessed |
| 6 | A fair ordinary-minimal-pointer/access control baseline can be frozen for the same task, and reconstruction burden can be measured. | Proposed legitimate control baseline and fixed burden fields: documents, prompts, explanations, corrected assumptions, steps, next-work identification. | Not assessed |
| 7 | Separate package/control Consumers can each meet fresh-Consumer requirements and remain unexposed pending later authorization. | Feasibility attestation only; no Consumer reservation or interaction at checklist stage. | Not assessed |
| 8 | Exact-run inputs, criteria, package/rendering/delivery boundaries, hashes/order, raw-output preservation, interventions, and independent evaluation can be frozen before output. | Feasibility map naming proposed evidence/control records; no artifacts need be constructed. | Not assessed |

Non-criteria: AI-roadmap domain, business/work domain, company/employer ownership, hosting location, Project size, and a particular vendor/repository are not selection requirements.  Their absence does not waive any mandatory row above.

**Owner selection decision:** A Project may be selected only when every row is established with adequate evidence and the Owner records the decision.  No candidate is identified by this checklist.

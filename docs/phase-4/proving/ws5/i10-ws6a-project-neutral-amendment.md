## 1. Decision and preserved original requirement

The original approved requirement remains historical evidence:

> “The Company AI Roadmap is the first external proving ground. Context Engine itself is the dogfooding case.” — Phase 0 §15.

Phase 1 Item 53, Phase 2 I4/I10/G12, and Phase 4 WS6A subsequently carried that named target.  The original I10 wording, “The proving Project is Company AI Roadmap,” remains preserved as the then-approved plan; it is not edited or treated as if it never existed.

**Superseded constraint:** the requirement that the external I10/WS6A proving Project specifically be **Company AI Roadmap**.

## 2. Effective replacement requirement

For all future I10/WS6A proving work, the proving Project shall be **one Project selected separately by the Project Owner that is real, independently maintained, non-synthetic, and other than Context Engine, and that satisfies every mandatory criterion in the frozen Project-Selection Eligibility Checklist.**

The Project’s AI-roadmap domain, business/work domain, company/employer ownership, hosting location, and size are not eligibility requirements.  The amendment removes only the named-project constraint.  It preserves I10’s fresh-Consumer, P1–P8, fair-control, reconstruction-burden, evidence-preservation, and independent-evaluation requirements, and it preserves WS6A as **NOT AUTHORIZED FOR EXECUTION**.

## 3. Mandatory eligibility criteria and proving invariants

Before any Consumer exposure, the separately selected Project must have evidence that:

1. it is a real, independently maintained Project other than Context Engine, not a synthetic fixture or proving-tailored corpus;
2. its exact identity, non-circular Bootstrap/configuration, authority basis, and Consumer-disclosure authority are established;
3. an authorized, observable, bounded task-relative Git/Markdown Source/ASU scope is available through supported v0.1 mechanisms, with exact scopes, observed versions/states, exclusions, and gaps recorded;
4. that scope supports a real governed continuation task covering I10’s required fields and a legitimate next activity without unauthorized work;
5. the frozen expected-state matrix maps actual evidence (or an expressly permitted accurate qualification/insufficient conclusion) to I10 P1–P8: current state, governance boundary, supersession, Candidate/proposal, Provenance, missing/unavailable/uncertain evidence, isolation, and adversarial Source content as evidence rather than instruction;
6. an Owner-approved, fair ordinary-minimal-pointer/access control baseline can be frozen for the same task and reconstruction burden can record the I10 measures: documents, clarification prompts, manual explanations, corrected assumptions, interaction steps, and identification of next legitimate work;
7. distinct package-arm and control-arm Consumers can each meet the applicable fresh-Consumer preflight and remain unexposed until their authorized exact deliveries; and
8. exact package/rendering, criteria, hashes/order, delivery, raw-original-output preservation, intervention records, and independent evaluation can all be frozen before the first Consumer output.

The task must continue to preserve authority, current/historical/superseded and Candidate/proposal distinctions, applicability/sufficiency, limitations/gaps, uncertainty, provenance, isolation, and no Project-specific core behavior.  Accurate qualified or insufficient conclusions remain valid only where the frozen source-specific matrix permits them.  No criterion permits artificial Source enrichment, answer/rubric leakage, coaching, privileged project logic, a single Consumer for both arms, or post-output alteration of criteria or evidence.

The concise application form is [Project-Selection Eligibility Checklist](project-selection-eligibility-checklist.md).  It contains no candidate Project, ranking, or selection.

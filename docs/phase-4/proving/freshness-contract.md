# Fresh-Consumer and Exact-Run Contract

**Status:** **FROZEN CONTROL — NO CANDIDATE RESERVED OR PREFLIGHTED BY THIS FILE.**

Freshness is reasonable run-specific, task-specific evidence that a Consumer
lacks material prior Project knowledge able to compensate for package defects.
A new chat alone is insufficient.

Before WS5.1, freeze eligibility, prohibited exposure, minimum identity and
provenance, reservation procedure, contamination conditions, and invalidation
conditions. WS5 exposes only reservation and approved preflight information;
it must not disclose WS6A task, package, expected answer, task-specific history,
or dogfooding material.

Before WS5.2, freeze procedure, allowed and prohibited inputs, expected
evidence, and PASS/FAIL/INDETERMINATE/INVALID criteria. Preserve raw preflight
evidence before evaluation. PASS retains an unexposed Consumer for later Owner
review. FAIL/INDETERMINATE stops and preserves. Invalid reservation is preserved
and cannot be silently substituted.

Future WS6A order: freeze task/control, permitted package, criteria, and run
identity; after separate authorization, use the fresh Consumer, deliver only
permitted context, preserve raw output/metadata, then independently evaluate.

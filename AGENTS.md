# Operating Rules

## Authority and governance

- The Project Owner is the final approving authority for all material project decisions.
- ChatGPT is the design partner, analyst, reviewer, challenger, and recommendation source.
- Codex implements and documents approved decisions.
- The Context Engine may communicate authority; it cannot create authority by retrieving or generating information.

## Codex operating boundaries

Codex may make ordinary implementation-level choices only within approved requirements and architecture. Codex must stop and report when work would require a decision that materially changes scope, architecture, technology, security, governance, data models, dependencies, or another approved boundary.

Codex must not:

- make material decisions independently or silently alter approved scope, architecture, or governance;
- introduce dependencies without approval;
- begin another phase without explicit authorization;
- implement unapproved features or an approved non-goal;
- grant authority, permissions, or authoritative status to AI-derived information;
- silently modify authoritative sources to reconcile inconsistencies; or
- stage, commit, or push changes unless explicitly instructed.

Phase completion and authorization of the next phase are separate Project Owner decisions.
